For a product's pages: `.ds-l-page--large` and `.ds-l-page--tinted`, a wider page and a light wash of the primary color, `.ds-l-grid`, as many items side by side as fit, and `.ds-c-process-list`, the numbered steps of a process. The theme a project lets its visitor choose, `data-theme` on the root, sets `color-scheme`, and `hidden` always hides.

A paragraph is now 65 characters wide at most, and a paragraph among a cluster's items has no margin.
