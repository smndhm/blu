Print styles: a printed page is in the light theme, the whole width of the sheet and its own height, without the header, footer and skip link; a heading is never left alone at the bottom of a sheet. `.ds-u-print-hidden` leaves out what only makes sense on screen, such as a download link. In a `hgroup`, the heading has no bottom margin: its tagline sits right under it, which the flex column kept apart (a CV position's dates; the home page's introduction comes up a little). A tagline in small print (a `<small>` alone in its paragraph, such as a date) has a line as low as its text. Every heading level has its size and margins, instead of the browser's: `h3` 20px, with 32px above and 8px below; `h4`, `h5` and `h6` the text's 16px (the base 4 has no step between 16px and 20px), set apart by the heading font and weight, with 24px above and 8px below. A `hgroup` with a `h2` or a `h3` takes its heading's margins, so that they collapse with its neighbors' as the heading's alone would.
