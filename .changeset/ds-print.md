Print styles: a printed page is in the light theme, the whole width of the sheet and its own height, without the header, footer and skip link; a heading is never left alone at the bottom of a sheet. `.ds-u-print-hidden` leaves out what only makes sense on screen, such as a download link. In a `hgroup`, the heading has no bottom margin: its tagline sits right under it, which the flex column kept apart (a CV position's dates; the home page's introduction comes up a little). A tagline in small print (a `<small>` alone in its paragraph, such as a date) has a line as low as its text. Every heading level is styled, two levels per size on the base 4, the higher one extra bold: `h1` 40px extra bold, `h2` 24px bold, `h3` 20px extra bold, `h4` 20px bold, `h5` 16px extra bold, `h6` the text's 16px bold; the browser's sizes and margins no longer show (articles, the CV). Printed, the page's main content has no padding of its own, the sheet's margins being enough. A `hgroup` with a `h2` or a `h3` takes its heading's margins, so that they collapse with its neighbors' as the heading's alone would.
