Print styles: a printed page is in the light theme, the whole width of the sheet and its own height, without the header, footer and skip link; a heading is never left alone at the bottom of a sheet. `.ds-u-print-hidden` leaves out what only makes sense on screen, such as a download link. In a `hgroup`, a `h2` to `h6` has no bottom margin: its tagline sits right under it, which the flex column kept apart (a CV position's dates); a `h1` keeps its own. A tagline in small print (a `<small>` alone in its paragraph, such as a date) has a line as low as its text. Every heading level is styled, on the base 4: `h1` 40px extra bold, `h2` 24px bold, `h3` 20px extra bold, and `h4`, `h5`, `h6` in the text's 16px, extra bold, bold and semibold; the browser's sizes and margins no longer show (articles, the CV). A heading right after another has no margin above: the previous heading's below sets the room between them (8px under a CV's company, 16px under a section's title). Printed, the page's main content has no padding of its own, the sheet's margins being enough. A `hgroup` with a `h2` to a `h6` takes its heading's margins, so that they collapse with its neighbors' as the heading's alone would. A new layout, `.ds-l-stack` (`layout/stack.css`): items one under the other, 8px apart, closer than paragraphs, such as a CV position's highlights. A board of typography, `tests/typography.html`, checked by the accessibility test with axe, each heading level's distinct style and screenshots in both themes; the README documents the typography.
