`.ds-u-text-center`: text centered, a paragraph across its container. `.ds-u-text-primary`: text in the primary color, such as the answer after a question in a heading. `--ds-c-hero-size-heading` sets a hero's heading size, as `--ds-c-hero-size-text` its text's. `.ds-c-card--horizontal`: an image (rounded) beside a few short lines, cut with an ellipsis when too long, such as an album's cover beside a link, then its title and artist. `.ds-l-cluster--center` puts a cluster's items in the middle of the line, and `.ds-l-cluster--equal` makes them as wide as each other across it, its images at their own size, such as two cards with an arrow between. A cluster of cards keeps as much room between its lines as between them. `.ds-l-page--tinted`'s glow is now in proportion to the window, lighter on a small one.
