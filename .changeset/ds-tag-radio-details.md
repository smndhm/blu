`.ds-c-tag`: a word or two beside a title, such as "New", small, bold, on the primary color, 16px high. `--ds-b-size-radius-small`: 4px, a small block's corners, such as a tag's. `.ds-c-radio`, `__control`, `__label`, `__description`: a native radio button and its label, as the checkbox, for the choices of a `fieldset`. In the base, without classes: `small` in 12px, for a mention that comes second; `fieldset` without the browser's border, its `legend` in bold over its fields, two of them apart; `summary` a pointer, its marker in the primary color, hovered and pressed as a link. The boards of states and typography show them.
